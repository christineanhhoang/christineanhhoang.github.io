How I work

I’ve worked in startups leading product development and in scaling companies integrating with client systems. Different challenges, same core principles.

01

Solving the right problems

I understand the real problem before jumping to solutions, working closely with product managers and engineers to explore constraints, timelines and opportunities — so the final experience is both feasible and impactful.

02

User-centred design thinking

I advocate for user research and regularly run usability testing. I place strong emphasis on validating design decisions through qualitative and quantitative data.

03

Decision making

Design can be pulled in different directions, especially with multiple stakeholders. I make informed decisions, balance competing needs, and keep projects moving forward with clarity and purpose.
