About me

I get huge satisfaction from designing services end to end: interviewing users, testing ideas, defining the scope and technical capabilities with product managers and engineers, and seeing the impact. I have also worked with design trade-offs against delivery constraints and adapted AI into my ways of working.

I am currently putting my product design skills into practice by testing a meal prep idea of my own. I’m in the discovery phase, researching how people approach meal planning through interviews and questionnaire responses.
